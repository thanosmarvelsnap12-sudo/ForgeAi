    Frequently asked questions

    Is every pack personalized?

    Yes. The GameKit experience is designed around customer-selected style, colors, name, and asset preferences. The exact customization options depend on the tier.

    Can I change my choices?

    Yes. The customizer includes a preview step so you can go back and edit your choices before final delivery.

    Are these official game products?

    No. GameKit is independent and uses original designs rather than third-party game logos, characters, or screenshots.

    How do digital products arrive?

    After checkout and the generation/delivery backend are connected, the buyer will receive the personalized files through the configured delivery flow.

    What happens if I don't receive my files?

    Use Order Help first. We'll attempt to restore access or resend the delivery. If the problem cannot be resolved, we'll review a refund request according to the published refund policy.
