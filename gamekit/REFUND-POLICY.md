# GameKit Delivery & Refund Policy

## Download problems
If a customer paid but cannot access the purchased files, GameKit will first attempt to restore or resend access.

## Technical problems
If a purchased file is corrupted, incomplete, or otherwise fails to work as described, GameKit will attempt to provide a working replacement.

## Refund requests
If the delivery or technical problem cannot reasonably be resolved, a customer may request a refund using the Order Help Center. Requests are reviewed using the order/payment information available from the payment provider.

## What customers should provide
- Email used at checkout
- Product purchased
- Order/payment ID, if available
- A short description of the problem
- Optional screenshot or other relevant evidence

Evidence is not required for every request. Customers should never upload passwords, full payment-card numbers, or unrelated personal information.

## Abuse and duplicate claims
GameKit may review repeated or inconsistent requests and may ask for additional information when reasonably necessary to verify a transaction.

This policy is a product-support framework, not a substitute for applicable consumer-protection law or the payment processor's rules. Applicable law and payment-provider requirements take precedence where required.
